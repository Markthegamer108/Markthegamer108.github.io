01Wii

Shroombrew

Shroombrew is a fan-made revival of the Wii Shop Channel. The patched channel talks to Shroombrew's own server instead of Nintendo's, so a real Wii can browse the catalog and install titles again. The catalog has official WiiWare and Virtual Console games, homebrew and Flash games. Wii Points cards are made on the site, and there are community pages for friend codes, gifts and points history.

02Nintendo WFC

WiiWave

WiiWave is a community revival for Nintendo WFC. It rewrites a disc image so the game talks to its own server instead of Nintendo's. Wii Chess is supported, Super Smash Bros. Brawl's patch is in testing, and Mario Kart Wii is next. The repo also has the weekly league, seasons and the server tooling behind them.

03tiptoi

RAVage

Converts mp3, wav, m4a or flac into the .rav files a Ravensburger tiptoi pen plays, and decrypts stock ones back to ogg. The converter runs in the browser, so nothing is uploaded. There are desktop builds too.

04Raspberry Pi

RaspberrySoft

Windows on a Raspberry Pi 5, built around the RP1 southbridge. It's a fleet of 20 KMDF drivers for the RP1 peripherals plus an imager that puts Windows 11 ARM64 on an SD card. The repo is private for now and slowly getting developed.

05Chess

Nexarch

Nexarch is a chess engine built from scratch. It's alpha-beta with an NNUE evaluation, magic bitboards and the usual search tricks, trained by self-play and graded by a panel of 32 engines. The goal is to beat Stockfish 19. It's not there yet, but Gen 3 is +300 Elo over Gen 2 and Gen 4 is training now.

06Internet

Wii Internet Channel with TLS

Wii Internet Channel with its real Opera 9.30 engine patched to speak modern HTTPS. TLS 1.2/1.3 with verified certificates are handled in-engine, with no proxy or server in the final design, and a QuickJS bridge renders modern JavaScript. Work in progress: HTTPS with a real CA bundle and TLS 1.3 verified in Dolphin, QuickJS rendering modern pages. Real-console testing is still pending.

07Photo

Photo Channel 2.0

Patched original Wii Photo Channel v3 with expanded image and video format support. The unchanged Nintendo UI can browse and view more 2D formats (PNG, BMP, TGA, GIF, WebP, TIFF, ICO and more), plus animated GIF/WebM/MP4/H.264 playback through the original movie player. Work in progress: Dolphin-verified up to v18, real-hardware verification still pending.
